## Dependencies

- `ox_lib`
- `ox_target`
- `ox_inventory`
- `oxmysql`
- A supported framework bridge

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the resource">
    Place `logical-crafting` inside your resources directory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install inventory items">
    Add every recipe ingredient, output item, and blueprint item to your inventory item definition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import SQL">
    Import the included SQL once if your release stores permanent blueprints or crafting progression.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review configuration">
    Configure the framework, target system, prop model, benches, and recipes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start in order">
    Ensure Logical Crafting after all dependencies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```cfg server.cfg
ensure ox_lib
ensure oxmysql
ensure es_extended
ensure ox_target
ensure ox_inventory
ensure logical-crafting
```

<Tip>
Use a test bench with one cheap recipe before loading your complete production recipe list.
</Tip>
