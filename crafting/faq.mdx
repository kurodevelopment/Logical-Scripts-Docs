<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The crafting bench does not appear">
    Verify the model name, coordinates, resource start order, and client console. Also confirm the bench is enabled in the configuration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The target option is missing">
    Confirm `ox_target` is started first and that your configured target resource name is correct.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Items show as missing even though I have them">
    Check exact lowercase item keys, inventory bridge selection, metadata requirements, and whether the item definitions loaded successfully.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The blueprint does not stay unlocked">
    Confirm the SQL table exists and the resource is saving against the correct character identifier.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
