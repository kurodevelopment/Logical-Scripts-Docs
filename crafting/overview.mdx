Logical Crafting provides configurable world benches, recipe progression, blueprint requirements, inventory validation, and a premium red-themed interface.

## Core features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Physical Benches" icon="anvil">Spawn or target configurable crafting stations in the world.</Card>
  <Card title="Blueprint Access" icon="scroll-text">Require permanent or inventory-based recipe unlocks.</Card>
  <Card title="Server Validation" icon="shield-check">Validate ingredients, permissions, and rewards server-side.</Card>
  <Card title="Flexible Recipes" icon="flask-conical">Configure duration, output amount, jobs, levels, and ingredients.</Card>
</CardGroup>
