**Logic Shop** is a configurable FiveM storefront resource with a responsive cart interface, item categories, multiple locations, cash and card checkout, optional stock limits, map blips, and business revenue support.

## Features

- ESX and QBCore support with automatic framework detection
- Optional `ox_inventory` integration with native framework inventory fallback
- Multiple shop types and locations from one configuration
- Item categories, quantities, prices, labels, and stock limits
- Cash and bank-card payment methods
- Server-side distance, price, stock, balance, and carry validation
- Optional business revenue deposits through popular banking resources
- Configurable blips, markers, interaction distance, currency, and branding
- Automatic refunds if an inventory delivery fails after payment

## Included shop presets

The supplied configuration includes convenience stores, liquor stores, phone stores, Ammu-Nation, a weed shop, an employee supply shop, and You Tool.

<Note>
The configured item names are server-specific. Every item sold by a shop must exist in your inventory system before players can purchase it.
</Note>

## How players use it

Players approach a configured location and press **E** by default. They can browse categories, add items to their cart, choose cash or card, and complete checkout inside the NUI.
