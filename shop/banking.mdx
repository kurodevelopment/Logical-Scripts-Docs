## Banking settings

```lua
Config.Banking = 'auto'
Config.DepositBusinessRevenue = true
Config.DefaultSocietyPrefix = 'society_'
```

Supported adapters:

- `okokBanking`
- `fd_banking`
- `Renewed-Banking`
- `tgg-banking`
- `wasabi_banking`
- `qb-banking`
- `qb-management`
- `esx_addonaccount`
- `none`

Set `Config.Banking = 'auto'` to select the first supported banking resource that is currently started.

## Connect a shop account

```lua
society = 'society_barber'
```

After a successful purchase, the full checkout total is sent to the configured society when business deposits are enabled.

<Note>
Leave `society = ''` when a shop should not deposit revenue into a business account.
</Note>

## ESX society prefix

For `esx_addonaccount`, Logic Shop automatically adds `Config.DefaultSocietyPrefix` when the supplied account does not already begin with `society_`.
